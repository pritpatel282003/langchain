**Notes**:  
- The plan cycles through Verbal Reasoning, Reading Comprehension, Mathematics Achievement, and Quantitative Reasoning to maintain engagement.  
- Topics are sequenced to respect prerequisites (e.g., foundational math before algebra, basic geometry before complex shapes).  
- Section assessments every 3 days include one full section (e.g., 35 min for Quantitative Reasoning) and one shortened section (e.g., 20 min for Mathematics Achievement) to fit within 60 minutes, with 5 minutes for test analysis.  
  - Full section timings: Verbal Reasoning (20 min, 34 questions), Reading Comprehension (35 min, 36 questions), Mathematics Achievement (40 min, 47 questions), Quantitative Reasoning (35 min, 38 questions).  
  - Shortened section timings: Mathematics Achievement (20 min, 25 questions), Quantitative Reasoning (15 min, 19 questions).  
- Full-length tests on Days 15, 30, 45, and 60 are 160 minutes with 160 questions, excluding the essay.  
- Difficulty progresses from Easy (Days 1–18) to Medium (Days 19–40 and review days) to build skills gradually.  
- Review days (41–59) revisit specific topics, prioritizing weak areas from full-length or section tests (aim for 70% accuracy). Use a notebook to track accuracy per topic.  
- “Quantitative Reasoning” is used instead of “Quantitative Comparison” to align with ISEE terminology.  
- Optional essay practice (30 min, 1 prompt) can be added on Days 14, 29, 44, or 59 if desired; consult your tutor to include this.
